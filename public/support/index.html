Frequently asked questions

A session didn't resume after I left the app

Reopen the app from where you left it; in-progress sessions are saved on your device and resume automatically. If the timer looks wrong, stop the session and start a new one.

I'm not getting reminder notifications

Reminders are optional, local notifications. Check that they're enabled in the app's settings and that notifications are allowed for the app in your device's system settings.

How do I reset my data?

All data lives on your device only. Deleting and reinstalling the app (iOS and Android), or clearing the site's storage in your browser (web), clears everything and starts fresh.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy for details — there's no account, no analytics, and no server.

Report a bug or request a feature

Found something broken, or have an idea for the app? Open an issue on GitHub, or just email us directly — whichever is easier for you.
