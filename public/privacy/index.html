Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · Applies to the iOS, Android, and web versions of the app

The short version: Meditation does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Meditation (“the app”) is developed and published by Oréliau Koumedzro (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@oreliaukmz.com.

Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and it does not create an account for you.

The app makes no network requests of its own. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or social-media SDKs, and it does not use tracking identifiers.

Data stored on your device

To work as a meditation timer, the app keeps a small amount of information in local storage on your device. This information never leaves the device and is not accessible to us.

WhatWhy
Onboarding choices (your intentions, experience level, and preferred soundscape)Remembered so onboarding is shown only once and to personalise future sessions
Timer settings and the state of a session in progressSo a session can resume after the app is backgrounded
Daily practice statistics (number and total duration of completed sessions)Shown as “Today’s practice” and in the optional evening summary
Program progress (which program sessions you have completed)So your place in a program is kept
Reminder preferencesTo schedule the local notifications you opted in to
Theme and language preferencesTo show the app the way you chose

You can erase all of this at any time by deleting the app (iOS and Android) or by clearing the site’s storage in your browser (web).

Notifications

The app can send a morning presence reminder, an evening summary, and an alert when a session ends while the app is in the background. All three are optional and off by default. They are local notifications: scheduled and delivered entirely by your device, without any push service or server. If you grant notification permission, it is used only for these reminders, and you can revoke it at any time in your device settings.

Device permissions

Beyond optional notifications, the app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health data, or any other sensitive capability. Haptic feedback and audio playback use standard device features and do not involve any data collection.

App stores and hosting

When you download the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may collect information (such as download counts, device type, or crash reports) under their own privacy policies and your device settings. We do not receive any personally identifiable information from them.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when the page is loaded; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not have access to, and do not use, these logs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal data, no data about children is collected either.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf; everything is under your control on your own device. If you have a privacy question or concern, email us at contact@oreliaukmz.com and we will answer as quickly as we can.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, if a future version added an optional sync feature — we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
