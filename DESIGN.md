# Aura Design System - Lumina Flow Meditation

## Overview

Zen Minimalism fused with Glassmorphism — an immersive, high-end sanctuary for mindfulness. The brand personality is serene, sophisticated, and ethereal, targeting a discerning audience seeking a digital escape as premium as a physical wellness retreat.

## Brand & Style

This design system embodies "Zen Minimalism" fused with "Glassmorphism" to create an immersive, high-end sanctuary for mindfulness.

### Visual Principles

- **Depth through Translucency:** Frosted glass effects stack information without breaking the user's connection to calming background visuals.
- **Breathable Composition:** Extreme use of negative space reduces cognitive load.
- **Soft Transitions:** Motion and layering mimic the fluid nature of breath and light.

### Color Palette

- **Deep Indigo (`primary-container`: #1A1B41):** The foundation. Used for deep backgrounds and primary surfaces to evoke the evening sky or deep water. In the light theme, the foundation becomes the pale tone `#E1E0FF`.
- **Soft Lavender (`secondary`: #CDBDFF / `primary`: #C2C2F2):** The accent. Used for primary actions, active states, and focus elements. It provides gentle, spiritual energy.
- **Mint Mist (`tertiary`: #B8CAC9):** The highlight. Used for high-contrast text and subtle iconography to provide a sense of fresh air and clarity.
- **Surfaces:** Dark theme backgrounds utilize a gradient of Deep Indigo to a darker Neutral (#0F0F1A) to create a sense of infinite space. Light theme surfaces use a warm, bright base (#FAF8FF).

### Typography

- **Headlines:** Playfair Display for titles, session names, and quotes. Typeset with generous leading to feel "airy."
- **UI Elements:** Inter for all functional data, navigation, and long-form descriptions.
- **Hierarchy:** Lowercase headlines feel more approachable; wide-tracked uppercase Labels provide a structured, premium look.

### Layout

**Fluid Grid** with wide margins creates a "sanctuary" feel where content is never crowded.

- **Desktop:** 12-column grid with 64px outer margins. Content centered in a max-width 1200px container.
- **Mobile:** 4-column grid with 24px margins. Vertical stacking with minimum 32px padding between sections.
- **Rhythm:** 8px base unit. Larger gaps (48px+) between functional groups.

### Elevation & Depth

Depth conveyed through "Glassmorphism" rather than traditional shadows.

- **Glass Layers:** Semi-transparent fill of Mint Mist (`tertiary`) or Lavender (`secondary`) at 5-10% opacity.
- **Backdrop Blur:** Heavy blur (20px to 40px) on all container backgrounds for a "frosted" effect.
- **Edge Definition:** 1px inner border (stroke) with 20% opacity white/Mint Mist (`tertiary`) on top and left edges.
- **Z-Index:** Two primary levels — Background (Immersive) and Glass Layer (Interactive).

### Shapes

Organic and soft, avoiding harsh 90-degree angles.

- **Containers:** `rounded-lg` (1rem)
- **Interactive Elements:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) or full pill shapes
- **Icons:** Thin-stroke (1.5px) with rounded terminals

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Lavender (`secondary`/#CDBDFF in dark, `primary` container in light) background with Indigo text. High roundedness.
- **Secondary (Glass):** Frosted glass background with Mint Mist (`tertiary`/#B8CAC9) border and text.
- **Play Button:** Large, circular glass element with subtle "pulse" animation.

### Cards
- Glassmorphism style with 24px internal padding
- Nested imagery with 8px rounded corners

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Underlined or ghost-input style (no background, only bottom border)
- **Selection:** Lavender glows for active states

### Navigation
- **Bottom Bar (Mobile):** Floating glass dock with high backdrop blur, detached from bottom edge

### Meditation Specifics
- **Progress Ring:** Thin, Mint Mist circular stroke that fills as session progresses
- **Audio Visualizer:** Gentle, low-opacity sine waves moving rhythmically with audio
